# Authoritative source entry points

Always verify current version, effective date and actual incorporation/binding basis.

## State law

- Grundgesetz: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/
- BGB: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/
- Anti-Doping-Gesetz: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/antidopg/

## DOSB

- DOSB Satzung und Grundsatzpapiere: https://www.dosb.de/ueber-uns/grundlagen-unserer-arbeit/satzung-und-grundsatzpapiere
- DOSB Safe Sport Code: https://www.dosb.de/themen/werte-des-sports/safe-sport/safe-sport-code

The DOSB Safe Sport Code is a model framework. Do not assume automatic binding for a club, federation, athlete, coach or other person. Verify incorporation in Satzung/Ordnung and, where needed, contractual or licensing submission.

## Anti-Doping

- NADA Nationaler Anti-Doping Code: https://www.nada.de/recht/nadc
- NADA templates for federation implementation: https://www.nada.de/recht/nadc/muster-fuer-verbaende

Treat NADC/WADC, a federation Anti-Doping-Ordnung and the AntiDopG as distinct legal/rule layers. Verify which version and dispute-resolution mechanism applies to the person and event.
