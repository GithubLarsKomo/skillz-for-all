# Authoritative source routing – Corporate Compliance Law

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- German Criminal Code (StGB), especially current anti-corruption provisions relevant to private commercial and public-official contexts.
- German Administrative Offences Act (OWiG), especially current organisational/supervisory-duty and corporate-fine provisions where applicable.
- Current German/EU sector-specific anti-fraud, procurement, grants, healthcare, transparency or integrity rules when triggered by the facts.
- Current foreign anti-bribery regimes only after a verified jurisdictional nexus; do not assume FCPA or UK Bribery Act applicability from international business alone.
- Internal Code of Conduct, gifts/hospitality, conflicts, third-party, sponsorship/donation and approval policies as organisational-policy evidence, not as statutory definitions.
- Investigation records, approvals, contracts, payment evidence, beneficial-ownership information and control records.

§ 130 OWiG is not a generic compliance-certification provision. Its application depends on the concrete underlying duty, required supervision and preventability/mitigation of the relevant violation. Private commercial bribery and public-official offences must be analysed separately.
