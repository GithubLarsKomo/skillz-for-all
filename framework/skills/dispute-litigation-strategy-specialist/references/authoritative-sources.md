# Authoritative source routing – Dispute / litigation strategy

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- Zivilprozessordnung (ZPO), current official text; verify current procedural version and deadlines for the specific posture.
- Gerichtsverfassungsgesetz (GVG) and other jurisdiction statutes as triggered.
- BGB/HGB and specialist substantive law for the actual claim/defense.
- Arbitration rules/agreement, foreign procedural law or international jurisdiction/enforcement instruments where applicable.
- Current court/authority decisions for material procedural or substantive issues.
- Contract, correspondence, records, expert material, witness evidence and preservation logs as factual evidence.

Court representation, formal procedural acts and binding litigation advice are routed to qualified counsel where required. Never invent a deadline, court competence or evidentiary rule from generic litigation practice.
