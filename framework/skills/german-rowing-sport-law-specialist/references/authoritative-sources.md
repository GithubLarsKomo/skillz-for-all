# Authoritative source entry points for German rowing

Always verify the current version and the version effective on the relevant event or decision date.

## Deutscher Ruderverband

- DRV Verband / Satzung entry point: https://www.rudern.de/verband
- DRV Satzung and orders: https://www.rudern.de/verband/ordnungen
- DRV competition rules and other rowing rules: https://www.rudern.de/regeln

The DRV rules page currently publishes the Ruder-Wettkampfregeln for 2026 and the DRV Anti-Doping-Ordnung; the orders page publishes the DRV Satzung and Rechts- und Verfahrensordnung. Treat these pages as discovery entry points rather than permanent edition pins.

## Anti-Doping

- NADA NADC: https://www.nada.de/recht/nadc
- German Anti-Doping Act: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/antidopg/

## International layer

If a matter invokes World Rowing/FISA rules, obtain the current official World Rowing rulebook and verify why it is incorporated into the concrete event or DRV procedure before relying on it.

## Evidence rule

Archive or reference the exact document/version used for a material conclusion. Do not infer current applicability from an old PDF filename, search result or prior season.
