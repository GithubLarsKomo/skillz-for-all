# Authoritative source entry points

Use these as discovery entry points and verify the current version and applicability at execution time.

- BGB, Vereinsrecht: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/ — especially the current provisions on associations beginning at § 21.
- Vereinsgesetz: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/vereinsg/
- Grundgesetz, especially Art. 9: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/
- Register evidence: use the competent official register/registry source for the specific association.
- Matter-specific statutes and rules: obtain the currently effective Satzung, Geschäftsordnungen, Wahlordnungen, Beitragsordnungen, disciplinary rules and resolutions from the association or official publication channel.

Do not copy legal conclusions from secondary summaries when a primary authority is available. Preserve version/as-of metadata and distinguish state law from private association rules.
