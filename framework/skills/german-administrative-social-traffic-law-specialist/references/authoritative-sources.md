# Authoritative source routing – German Administrative, Social & Traffic Law

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- Current federal/state administrative procedure and court rules applicable to the specific authority and matter.
- VwVfG/VwGO where applicable, plus the relevant specialist statute.
- Current Social Code books (SGB) and SGG for social-law matters.
- Current StVG, FeV and specialist traffic/fahrerlaubnis rules for traffic-administrative matters.
- Official notices, hearing letters, file excerpts, expert opinions, registry information and service evidence as primary matter documents.

Administrative and social-law remedies are highly matter- and document-specific. Never infer jurisdiction, admissibility or deadlines from a generic public-law template.
