# Authoritative source routing – Finance, Insolvency & Restructuring Law

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- Insolvenzordnung (InsO), current text and relevant insolvency-application, avoidance, ranking, creditor and procedure provisions.
- Unternehmensstabilisierungs- und -restrukturierungsgesetz (StaRUG), current text.
- Current BGB/HGB/corporate-law rules for financing, guarantees, surety, securities, authority and capital-maintenance issues.
- Current EU/cross-border insolvency and conflict-of-law rules when foreign entities/assets/creditors are involved.
- Financing agreements, waivers, covenant certificates, security documents, forecasts, liquidity evidence, board records and creditor correspondence as matter evidence.

Do not infer insolvency status from one KPI or historic guidance. Current statutory tests, forecast horizons, responsible persons and verified financial evidence must be assessed for the concrete entity and date.
