# Source quality for product evidence

## Claim-relative hierarchy

Quellenqualität wird relativ zum Claim bewertet. Herstellerseiten sind primär für eigene Spezifikationen; unabhängige, methodisch transparente Tests sind stärker für vergleichende Performance; Nutzerberichte sind meist kontextuell für Langzeiterfahrung und Failure Modes.

## Channel guidance

| Channel | Typical strength | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturer | primary | marketing/comparative bias |
| Specialist magazine | strong-secondary when methodology is transparent | affiliate/editorial incentives |
| Independent lab / consumer organisation | strong-secondary | scope and model revision |
| Retailer | primary/contextual for offer data | sales incentive |
| User reviews | contextual | selection, manipulation, survivorship bias |
| YouTube | method-dependent | sponsorship, affiliate incentives |
| Technical literature | primary/strong-secondary | transferability to exact SKU/use |
| Regulator/certifier | primary | only for the regulated/certified claim |

## Anti-bias rules

Do not confuse popularity, review count, brand familiarity, launch recency or MSRP with quality. Keep sponsorship and affiliate status visible. Deduplicate syndicated evidence through `evidenceFamilyId`.

## Negative evidence

Repeated independent reports of the same failure mode increase confidence. A single anecdote does not establish a systematic defect. Recalls, support discontinuations and official service limitations are direct evidence for the relevant claim.
