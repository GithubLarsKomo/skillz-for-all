# Authoritative source routing – Tax legal interface

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime legal/tax verification remains matter-specific.

- Steuerberatungsgesetz (StBerG), especially §§ 2–6 for professional authorization boundaries.
- AO, EStG, KStG, GewStG, UStG, GrEStG, ErbStG and international/treaty sources only when the concrete Tax Specialist confirms relevance.
- Official BMF, Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, tax authority and court sources for current-law research.
- Transaction documents, ownership charts, cash flows, accounting/tax records and existing rulings/positions as factual inputs.

This skill identifies and structures tax issues. It must not convert general research into an individualized binding tax position where an authorized Tax Professional is required.
