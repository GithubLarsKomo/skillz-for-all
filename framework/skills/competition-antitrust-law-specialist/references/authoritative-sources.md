# Authoritative source routing – Competition / Antitrust

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- Gesetz gegen Wettbewerbsbeschränkungen (GWB), including § 1, §§ 18–21 and merger-control provisions when triggered.
- Articles 101 and 102 TFEU and Regulation (EC) No 1/2003 for EU competition-law application/enforcement.
- Current EU block-exemption regulations and Commission guidelines only where their conditions and current effective version are actually satisfied.
- Bundeskartellamt, European Commission, CJEU/BGH and other competent authority/court materials for current interpretation and enforcement practice.
- Transaction documents, market data, competitor roles and actual communications as factual evidence.

Never infer exemption, safe harbour, dominance or merger-control filing status from a clause label, NDA, market-share guess or outdated threshold table.
