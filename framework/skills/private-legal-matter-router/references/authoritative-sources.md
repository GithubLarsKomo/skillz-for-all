# Authoritative source routing – Private legal matters

Discovery baseline verified 2026-08-28. Runtime re-check via `current-law-context` is mandatory.

- RDG §§ 1–6 for the German scope/authorization distinction around concrete third-party legal services; § 2 Abs. 3 Nr. 6 concerns matters within affiliated companies and must not be projected onto private matters.
- Applicable procedural statutes for court/authority representation and deadlines in the concrete domain.
- BGB and specialist statutes for the actual private-law matter.
- Insurance terms/policy, court/authority documents, contracts, notices and correspondence as factual evidence.
- StBerG professional authorization boundaries for foreign tax matters via `tax-legal-interface-specialist`.

The router does not infer that AI support, self-help, external legal service, authorized representation or professional tax advice have identical legal status. It routes the concrete action to the proper specialist/authority.
